Saaf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Your photos never leave your device. We show ads, which share standard device identifiers with Google — nothing else.

What Saaf accesses

With your permission, Saaf reads the photos and videos on your phone to show them as cards. It reads file details (size, date, folder, dimensions) and creates small preview images and similarity fingerprints. All of this is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Photos and videos are accessed but never collected, uploaded or shared. Saaf has no server and no account.

Deleting files

Saaf never deletes anything without your confirmation. Items you bin go to Android's own trash, where you can restore them until they are permanently deleted (after up to 30 days, or sooner if you empty the bin).

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and approximate location, and shares them with Google. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent first and can change your choice under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

If you buy "Remove ads", the ad SDK is not started at all and no advertising data is sent.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. Saaf only learns whether you own it; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Saaf is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights and contact

Because Saaf holds no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. For questions or grievances, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, contact our grievance officer at contact@locleo.com.
